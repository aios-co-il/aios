# Premium style catalog

Use this catalog for concept boards. Select directions that fit the brief; do not force all ten when the user asks for fewer.

| Direction              | Visual system                                                      | Hebrew typography                                      | Best fit                                | Avoid                         |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Swiss editorial        | Strict asymmetric grid, cream/black, one brand accent, thin rules  | Oversized geometric sans, tight leading                | Professional services, culture, travel  | Decorative clutter            |

| Luxury minimal         | Warm neutral field, deep charcoal/navy, restrained metallic detail | Refined serif headline with clean sans support         | Premium travel, real estate, beauty     | Excessive gold or shadows     |

## Diversity rule

In a 10-tile board, include at least:

- three typography-led directions;
- three image- or object-led directions;
- two tactile/material directions;
- two futuristic or digital directions.

Do not repeat the same hero composition, background family, type scale, or CTA treatment more than twice.
